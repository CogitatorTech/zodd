About Zodd

Zodd is a small embeddable Datalog engine written in pure Zig. This page runs the engine compiled to WebAssembly, entirely in your browser.

The Datalog frontend supports recursive rules, stratified negation (not), aggregates (count, sum, min, and max), and integer or string constants. Programs with ?- queries answer them; otherwise all derived relations are printed.
